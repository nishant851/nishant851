About

I'm a third-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at NMIET, Talegaon Dabhade. I like projects where AI does something people can feel: a voice app that understands unclear speech, a classifier that filters spam, a pump that waters plants when the soil gets dry.

I like to get an idea running quickly, often as a single HTML file, then improve it. Outside code I design interfaces in Figma and read up on ethical hacking.
